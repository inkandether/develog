Every cave has its own tune

Until this week, a cave in Tabby Trails sounded exactly like the meadow above it. The cat would climb down a ladder into a dark side-on world of ledges, bats and torches, and Cat Radio would carry on cheerfully playing Acorn Path, as if nothing had happened. That always felt a bit like going down into a cellar with your headphones on.

Now every cave plays one of five tunes from the moment the cat starts down the ladder until it climbs back out: Two Notes in the Dark, Glow-worm Lullaby, Something Big Below, The Crystal Hall and Tiptoe Past the Lair. Which one a cave gets comes from the world's seed and where its mouth is, so it's always the same tune in the same cave, for everyone in that world. The one in the clip is Glow-worm Lullaby, which is a music box playing a little waltz over a clock that ticks along with it, and a bass that sinks a half step every bar.

Each tune has three stages. The game measures how far the cat has come from the ladder, along and down both counting, so a long flat tunnel builds as well as a deep shaft does, and at a third of the way and again at two thirds the tune moves up. What that means differs from tune to tune. Glow-worm Lullaby plays the whole thing a half step higher and a bit quicker each time. Something Big Below starts with drums muffled as if they're behind the rock and brings them nearer. Two Notes in the Dark is two bass notes a half step apart, like something large breathing, and they just come closer together until they're running. It only ever goes up during a visit, so backing off a little doesn't let the tension out.

They're all played by one little cave band that can sit right in front of you, off the far wall as an echo, or behind the rock, muffled, so the echo is part of the arrangement rather than an effect laid over the top.

It's all live, and the game runs in the browser.

Also lately

  • Invite codes are words now (something like MOSSY OTTER 47), and a cat the world doesn't know knocks: the owner gets KNOCK KNOCK, with LET IN or NOT NOW.
  • A bicycle leaves one line in the snow and the sand, a skateboard two, broken where a trick lifts the board.
  • Hold Z or X with a snowball and a dotted arc creeps out ahead, the way a pounce aims; let go and it lands there.
  • A sproutling has one GATHER command instead of MINE and CHOP: it mines the rock in front of it and chops the tree.
  • In story mode every dish and bake heals three hearts, and a night in bed one or two.
  • Animals in a pen are saved with the world, and a fenced-in herd no longer doubles every time the cat comes back.
  • The game's sprites are painted once when it's built instead of in every player's browser, so the first frame comes in about a third of the time on my laptop, and half on a slow phone.
  • On a touch screen the big button says PAW, and the other one says what it will do: CODE facing a sproutling, SLEEP facing a bed.
  • A Suit and Tie (two cloth and a wool), and caps and capes go on over the frog and raccoon suits.
  • A wolf's bite throws yellow and white stars off the cat, instead of red bits.
